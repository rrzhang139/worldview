# Sources — Curated Allowlist

The discipline of this repo is in this file. Pulling from anywhere not on this list defeats the purpose. If a source feels too noisy, remove it. If something important is missing repeatedly, add it.

## AI Capabilities & Models

- **Lab technical reports / system cards** (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta AI) — go to the primary source on every significant release. Skip the press release; read the evals section.
- **Epoch AI** (epochai.org) — quantitative tracking of compute trends, training runs, parameter counts. The authoritative data source on "are we still on the scaling curve?"
- **Dwarkesh Patel podcast** — long-form interviews with AI lab principals, researchers, and macro thinkers who intersect with AI. Best signal on what frontier researchers actually believe.
- **Semianalysis (Dylan Patel)** — the deepest public analysis on AI infrastructure economics: training cluster design, inference costs, GPU supply chains. Primary source for semis × AI.
- **arXiv (selectively)** — only when a paper is from a major lab AND its methodology is novel (new architecture, new training paradigm, new capability). Don't chase every preprint.

## Semiconductors & China Tech

- **Semianalysis** — also the best source on TSMC yields, SMIC progress, packaging bottlenecks, HBM dynamics.
- **CSET (Center for Security and Emerging Technology)** — rigorous research on China chip progress, export control effectiveness, talent flows.
- **The Information (China bureau)** + **Reuters tech desk** — breaking news on SMIC, Huawei Ascend, Chinese AI lab funding.
- **Caixin Global** — Chinese business press in English; useful for reading between the lines on SMIC/Huawei.
- **TSMC, ASML, NVDA, AMD earnings calls (transcripts)** — CC Wei's comments on demand, yield, packaging are the most reliable public read on what's actually constrained.

## AI Policy & Regulation

- **BIS Federal Register announcements** (commerce.gov/bis) — the primary source for export control rule changes. Subscribe to alerts.
- **EU AI Office** (ai-act.eu) — GPAI model registration, implementation timelines, enforcement.
- **CSET policy briefs** — best analysis of compute governance, export control design, China AI policy.
- **Lawfare blog** — AI governance and national security law intersection.
- **Congressional testimony transcripts** — Armed Services, Commerce, HSGAC committees. Read the lab CEO testimonies directly; the questions reveal what policymakers are afraid of.

## Tech Strategy & Investments

- **Stratechery (Ben Thompson)** — strategy + capital allocation in tech. Best lens for "where does value accrue in the stack."
- **The Information** — primary reporting on lab burn rates, cloud deals, M&A. Paywalled but high signal.
- **Hyperscaler 10-Qs and earnings calls** — MSFT, GOOG, META, AMZN. The capex guidance and AI revenue commentary are load-bearing for the investment thesis.

## Energy (AI-relevant only)

- **World Nuclear News** — nuclear restart progress, SMR milestones, PPA announcements.
- **IEA data center energy tracking** — the primary source for AI power demand projections.
- **Hyperscaler sustainability reports + PPA announcements** — where MSFT/GOOG/META lock in energy tells you how tight the constraint is.

## Geopolitics (chip-relevant)

- **CSIS China Power Project** — Taiwan strait readiness, PLA capability assessments, chip dependency tracking.
- **War on the Rocks** — defense / strategic studies long form when AI capability intersects with national security.

## What to avoid

- **AI news aggregators** (The Decoder, VentureBeat, TechCrunch AI) — usually press release rewrites. Go to the lab's tech report or The Information directly.
- **Twitter/X trending** — noise machine. Useful only as a pointer to primary source; never cite it directly.
- **Macro commentary** (FT Alphaville, CNBC, Bloomberg TV) — we've narrowed out of macro; the signal-to-noise on this beat is low for our themes.
- **arXiv firehose** — 500+ AI papers a week; 490 are incremental. Filter hard.
