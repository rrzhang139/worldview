# Sources — Curated Allowlist

The discipline of this repo is in this file. Pulling from anywhere not on this list defeats the purpose. If a source feels too noisy, remove it. If something important is missing repeatedly, add it.

## Macro / Finance

- **Financial Times** — front page, "The Big Read", Alphaville (Robin Wigglesworth, Bryce Elder). Skip celebrity/lifestyle.
- **The Economist** — "Finance & economics" section, "Briefing" essays, "Free exchange" column. Skip "The world this week" (headline summary).
- **Matt Levine — Money Stuff** (Bloomberg newsletter) — capital markets plumbing, regulation, weird-finance signal.
- **BIS Quarterly Review** — Bank for International Settlements; the place for cross-border banking + global liquidity.
- **IMF World Economic Outlook + Fiscal Monitor** (semi-annual). Use the data tables, not the press release.
- **Federal Reserve press conferences + minutes**, **PBOC quarterly monetary report**, **ECB monetary policy statement**. Primary sources only.

## Geopolitics

- **Foreign Affairs** (long form, monthly) — for structural shifts, not breaking news.
- **War on the Rocks** — defense / strategic studies long form.
- **CSIS commentary** (csis.org) — for industrial policy, Taiwan, defense readiness.
- **Tracker sources for conflict status:** ISW (Ukraine), CSIS China Power Project, ACLED dataset.

## China

- **Bill Bishop — Sinocism** newsletter — daily-but-curated read on Chinese policy / state media signals.
- **MERICS** (Mercator Institute for China Studies) — German think tank, structural research.
- **Trivium China** — economic policy translation.
- **Caixin Global** — Chinese business press in English.

## AI / Tech

- **Stratechery (Ben Thompson)** — strategy + capital allocation in tech.
- **Dwarkesh Patel podcast** — long interviews with AI/energy/macro principals.
- **Semianalysis (Dylan Patel)** — semis, AI infra, training/inference economics.
- **Epoch AI** — quantitative tracking of compute, parameters, training cost.
- **The Information** — primary tech reporting, paywalled but high signal.
- **Lab papers (DeepMind, Anthropic, OpenAI tech reports)** — read the system cards / model cards directly when capabilities shift.

## Energy / Commodities

- **IEA reports** (World Energy Outlook, monthly oil market report).
- **EIA STEO** (US Energy Information Administration short-term outlook).
- **Goehring & Rozencwajg** quarterly commentary — commodity supercycle thesis.
- **Adam Rozencwajg, Doomberg** — energy/commodity newsletters with strong priors (read with grain of salt but informative).

## Data dashboards (machine-readable)

- **FRED** (St. Louis Fed) — yields, FX, prices.
- **TradingEconomics** for global macro snapshots.
- **CEIC / NBS** for China data.
- **Bloomberg terminal** if available.

## What to avoid

- **Twitter/X frontpage, HN, Reddit, /r/wallstreetbets** — noise machines. Use only as pointers, never as primary sources.
- **CNBC / cable financial TV** — the financial equivalent of headline news.
- **"AI news" aggregators** — usually press release rewrites. Go to the lab's tech report directly.
- **Macro influencers without books or institutional history** — high noise to signal.
