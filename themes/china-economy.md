# China Economy

## Question
Is China successfully transitioning from a property-and-investment-led growth model to a consumption + advanced-manufacturing model — or stuck in a balance-sheet recession?

## Why it matters
China is the second-largest economy and the swing factor for global commodities, manufacturing supply, and EM debt. Whether it hits stall speed (Japan-1990) or finds a new growth model determines a lot about the next decade — global rates, commodity demand, EM equity, US-China tension, and the "is the dollar system threatened" question.

## What would change my mind
- **Bull pivot triggers:** sustained recovery in household consumption / retail sales, household credit demand returning, property starts stabilizing for 2+ quarters, real estate developer financing reopening, sustained CPI > 2%.
- **Bear deepening triggers:** local government financing vehicle (LGFV) defaults cascading, deflation persisting > 4 quarters, capital flight visible in FX reserves, banking system NPL recognition forced.
