# Geopolitics & Active Conflicts

## Question
Is the post-1991 unipolar liberal order ending, and what does the multipolar configuration that replaces it actually look like — alliance structures, conflict frequency, trade fragmentation?

## Why it matters
The arc of geopolitics determines whether globalization continues to deepen or fragments into blocs (US/EU/allies vs. China/Russia + non-aligned). That arc shapes supply chains, defense spending, semiconductor policy, sanctions reach, and the dollar's reserve status. Active conflicts (Ukraine, Middle East, Taiwan flashpoints) are the visible tests of the new configuration.

## What would change my mind
- **Fragmentation-deepening triggers:** Taiwan blockade or kinetic action; NATO Article 5 invocation; sanctions secondary enforcement on China; collapse of WTO dispute settlement; further BRICS+ expansion with substance (not just symbolism).
- **Stable-multipolar triggers:** Ukraine ceasefire that holds; Middle East de-escalation; Taiwan strait routinely transited without incident; trade volumes recover to pre-2018 trajectory.
