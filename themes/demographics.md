# Demographics & Labor

## Question
How fast and how disruptively are the demographic transitions in China, Japan, Korea, Europe, and (eventually) India playing out — and how does AI-driven productivity / immigration policy modulate the macro outcome?

## Why it matters
Demographics is the slowest, most ignored, and most powerful structural variable. China's working-age population peaked in 2014; Korea's TFR is below 0.8; Japan has been here for 30 years; Europe is heading there. The US is the demographic outlier in DM, but only because of immigration — which is now politically contested. Demographics drives long-run growth, savings/investment balance, real rates, and the political economy of immigration and AI labor displacement.

## What would change my mind
- **AI-as-substitute triggers:** measurable productivity acceleration in aging-DM economies despite labor force decline; firms reporting headcount stable while output grows materially.
- **Demographic-doom triggers:** Chinese pension system insolvency forcing unpopular reform; Japan-style 0% growth becoming Korean / Italian baseline; persistent dependency-ratio fiscal stress across DM.
