# Tech Strategy & Investments

## Question
How are the strategic positions of the dominant tech firms (hyperscalers, foundation model labs, vertical AI applications, semis) shifting as the AI capex cycle plays out — who's building moats, who's being commoditized, and where's value accruing in the stack?

## Why it matters
Strategy in tech right now is more contested than at any time since the cloud transition. The model layer is being commoditized faster than any prior software category. Distribution is increasingly the moat — but distribution costs (default placements, OEM deals) are rising. Hyperscalers are simultaneously customers and competitors of the labs they fund. Vertical AI applications are racing horizontal players. Understanding where value accrues (chips? cloud? models? apps? data?) is the difference between predicting which businesses thrive vs. just observing.

## What would change my mind
- **Lab-moat-confirming triggers:** OpenAI / Anthropic show pricing power despite open-source catching up; consumer products with real retention (not novelty); enterprise contracts with multi-year ramps locking in.
- **Commoditization triggers:** open-source frontier within 6 months of closed; price wars on inference deepen below cost; lab gross margins compress; hyperscalers internalize their own inference (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium taking real share from NVDA).
