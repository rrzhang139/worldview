# China Tech

## Question
Can China close the gap on advanced semiconductors, AI, and frontier hardware under US export controls — and if so, how fast and through what pathway (smuggling, indigenous innovation, regulatory arbitrage)?

## Why it matters
This is the bilateral fault line in the next decade of geopolitics. If China achieves credible domestic 5nm-class manufacturing, the US export-control regime collapses politically. If they don't, the AI race bifurcates and Chinese AI labs become structurally compute-limited. The outcome cascades into Taiwan strategic value, semiconductor capex globally, and US industrial policy intensity.

## Mental model

**Constraints create parallel stacks.**

Export controls don't stop capability — they redirect it. Constraint forces innovation along *different* axes (different lithography paths, chip architectures, supply chains). Read every China-tech development asking: *is China catching up on the same stack, or building a different stack?* The first scenario means the export controls are working temporarily; the second means parallel optimization is happening — and that's more dangerous structurally because it makes Chinese capability harder to evaluate through Western metrics. **Both outcomes are concerning in different ways**: catching up means controls fail eventually; parallel stack means the West loses ability to read the leaderboard.
