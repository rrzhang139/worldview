# Dollar & Reserve Dynamics

## Question
Is the dollar's role as global reserve currency eroding at the margin — and if so, through what mechanism (BRICS payment systems, gold accumulation, bilateral local-currency settlement, sanctions-driven flight)?

## Why it matters
The dollar's reserve status underwrites US deficit financing capacity, sanctions reach, and the unique privilege of issuing the global safe asset. Erosion is slow and lumpy, not a binary event. But the second-order consequences (higher US borrowing costs, fragmented payment systems, gold as monetary asset) reshape every other theme.

## What would change my mind
- **Erosion-accelerating triggers:** central bank gold buying sustains > 1,000 tonnes/yr for multiple years; BRICS payment system processes meaningful share of cross-border trade settlement; dollar share of allocated reserves drops below 55% (currently ~58%); a major oil exporter prices long-term contracts in non-USD.
- **Status-stable triggers:** dollar share of reserves stable in 58-60% band; gold inflows decelerate; foreign holdings of Treasuries grow with deficits.
