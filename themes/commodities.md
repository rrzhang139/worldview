# Commodities

## Question
Are we in a structural commodity bull cycle driven by underinvestment + electrification + fiscal-led demand — or is this just cyclical noise on top of a fundamentally well-supplied world?

## Why it matters
Commodities are real-economy substrate. Oil price determines inflation pass-through; copper price gates electrification; uranium gates nuclear restart; critical minerals gate the green/AI transitions. Years of underinvestment in mining capex (~50% off 2012 peak) collide with demand from AI data center power, EVs, grid buildout, and reshoring.

## What would change my mind
- **Supercycle-confirming triggers:** sustained backwardation in copper, oil, uranium curves; producer capex still subdued through high prices (capital discipline holding); EM real growth surprising up; OPEC+ unable/unwilling to add barrels.
- **Cycle-not-supercycle triggers:** EV demand growth disappoints copper thesis; OPEC+ adds material spare capacity back; mining capex catches up faster than expected; recession in DM demand.
