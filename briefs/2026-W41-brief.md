# Weekly Brief — 2026-W41

*A thin week on primary sources: one hard data print (TSMC), two carry-forward policy stories with no resolution. Nothing triggers a mind-changer.*

## Top developments this week

1. **TSMC September revenue: NT$511.86B, -0.6% MoM, +54.6% YoY; Q3 beats the top of guidance.** Q3 totals ~NT$1,494B against a US$44.6–45.8B guide (~NT$1,427–1,466B at NT$32); YTD +41.1%. **Updates** `themes/semiconductors.md`: the first MoM dip after four record months sits on a plateau, which reads as capacity-limited (CoWoS fully booked) rather than demand fading. The Oct 16 Q3 call is the next read on the Q4 guide.

2. **FY2027 NDAA chip-export provisions still unresolved in conference.** The Senate manager's amendment carries the Chip Security Act (location verification on exported chips); ITIC has asked conferees to reject it along with the AI OVERWATCH and MATCH Acts. **Updates** `themes/ai-policy-regulation.md`: Congress remains the only live venue for tightening, and last year's GAIN AI precedent says watch for stripping.

3. **China's Nvidia RTX Pro 5500 opening stays a signal, not a decision.** MIIT is reportedly surveying buyers; no approval, delivery or US licence confirmed, and the ~1M-unit figure lacks wire corroboration. **Updates** `themes/china-tech.md`: W40's read holds.
