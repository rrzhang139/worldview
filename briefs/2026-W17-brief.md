# Weekly Brief — 2026-W17

## Top developments this week

1. **Hyperscaler 2026 capex guides confirm ~$700B (+36% YoY), but FCF compression is now visible.** AMZN $200B, GOOG $175–185B, META $115–135B, MSFT $110–120B. About 75% / ~$450B is AI-specific. *The new wrinkle:* Meta's free cash flow projects ~-90%, Amazon expects ~-$17B FCF for 2026 — first time the AI cycle's cash dynamics have turned this stark. The narrative has flipped from "Wall Street rewarding capex on faith" to "Wall Street demanding evidence of monetization." Q1 hyperscaler earnings on Apr 28 → May 1 is the regime test for whether capex sustainability holds. Updates `themes/ai-compute-capex.md` (capex magnitude is real, but the bubble-watch lens is now active) and `themes/tech-investments.md` (investor scrutiny shift is the structural change).

2. **TSMC Q1 (Apr 16): blowout + bottleneck reframe.** Revenue +35.1% YoY (record), profit +58%, full-year 2026 guide raised to >30% growth. HPC/AI = 61% of revenue; advanced nodes (<7nm) now 74% of wafer revenue. *The reframe:* >10% of 2026 capex now going to advanced packaging (CoWoS, InFO, SoIC). Translation: the binding constraint has moved off raw wafer fabrication and onto **advanced packaging + the HBM complex.** This is the supply-side counterweight to the demand-side scrutiny in #1 — TSMC sees no demand crack. Updates `themes/semiconductors.md`.

3. **⚠️ Middle East energy shock is rewriting the macro path.** The Strait of Hormuz / US-Iran standoff drove a *bear-flattening* of the curve over the intermeeting period — but importantly, the 2Y rose primarily on **inflation compensation**, not real-rate expectations. Market-implied path now sees only ~1 cut in 2026 (down from earlier). FOMC meets Apr 28-29 and is expected to hold at 3.50–3.75%. The transmission chain is the cleanest live example of cross-pillar dynamics: geopolitics → oil → inflation breakevens → 2Y yield → Fed reaction function → equity multiples. Updates `themes/geopolitics-conflicts.md`, `themes/monetary-policy.md`, `themes/commodities.md`.

4. **China: 11th LPR hold + Q1 GDP +5% + CPI projected back to +0.9% in 2026.** PBOC keeping powder dry while weighing Middle East fallout against resilient growth and *fading* deflation. This is a modest positive — the first foothold of the bull-pivot trigger list (deflation easing). Property stabilization still gradual, tier-1-only. Updates `themes/china-economy.md`.

5. **Gold consolidates while DXY stays soft.** Gold ~$4,728, down ~15% from the Jan ATH of $5,595, on higher real yields + opportunity cost. But DXY at 98.57 is still ~14% below the 2022 peak despite a hawkish Fed and an energy shock that should be dollar-supportive. The micro-divergence is interesting — gold cooling doesn't mean reserve diversification thesis is over; it means the asset positioning got crowded and is unwinding into stronger yields. Updates `themes/dollar-reserves.md`.
