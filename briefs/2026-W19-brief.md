# Weekly Brief — 2026-W19

## Top developments this week

1. **⚠️ Anthropic leases all of SpaceX/xAI's Colossus 1 — a frontier lab is now buying compute from a rival lab.** Announced May 6 at Code with Claude (SF). Terms: 220K+ NVIDIA GPUs (H100/H200/GB200), 300+ MW capacity, online within the month. xAI moved its own training to Colossus 2; the deal is timed ahead of xAI's June IPO. Anthropic also stated intent to develop *multi-gigawatt orbital AI compute* with SpaceX. **The structural read:** the lab/hyperscaler/specialty-cloud lines are collapsing into a tradable compute layer. Anthropic effectively did an M&A-on-capacity instead of a hyperscaler deal — because the binding constraint on its product (Claude Pro/Max user growth) was capacity, not algorithms or money. Two implications: (a) compute is more fungible than ideology — Musk publicly reversed prior criticism of Anthropic in the same week; (b) a frontier lab hit cloud-allocation ceilings *faster* than the AWS/MSFT/GOOG capex buildout is releasing them. Updates `themes/ai-compute-capex.md` (mind-changer flagged), `themes/tech-investments.md`, `themes/ai-models-economics.md`, `themes/energy-systems.md`.

2. **Code with Claude — Anthropic launches Orbit + doubled Claude Code rate limits.** Orbit is a *proactive* assistant that auto-syncs Gmail/Slack/GitHub/Figma to deliver personalized briefings — explicitly targeted at "product creators" (devs, designers, PMs), not exec inbox-readers. Also announced: "dreaming" (between-session memory consolidation), Claude managed agents / multi-agent orchestration, Code with Claude routines. Doubled Claude Code 5h rate limits; removed peak-hours throttling on Pro/Max. Conference dates: SF May 6, London May 19, Tokyo June 10. **Read through tech-investments mental model:** *value migrates up the stack until distribution becomes the moat.* Anthropic is making a direct distribution play — moving from "API + Claude.ai" toward an ambient assistant inside the workflow tools developers already use. The race for "who owns the developer relationship" (Cursor, Anthropic, GitHub/MSFT, Google) is now openly contested at the application layer. Updates `themes/tech-investments.md`.

3. **Hyperscaler concentration in IG corporate debt index doubled in 12 months — 2.2% → 4.1%.** UBS now estimates $230–240B hyperscaler bond issuance in 2026 (vs. $121B in 2025; Oracle $18B and Alphabet $20B-incl-100yr-GBP-bond are recent prints). The capex bill is no longer just an equity story — it's a credit-market concentration risk and, by extension, a passive-bond-fund exposure that index investors are silently long. **The new test for the bubble-watch trigger** on `themes/ai-compute-capex.md`: do IG spreads widen if any AI-revenue stumble shows up at NVDA earnings May 20 or in Q2 hyperscaler prints? If concentration matters, even a modest spread-widening would compound mechanically into higher cost of next-round capex financing. Updates `themes/ai-compute-capex.md`, `themes/tech-investments.md`.

4. **Huawei Ascend 910C yield hits ~40% (profitability threshold) and 2028 roadmap places Ascend 950PR on SMIC's N+3 with Huawei-developed HBM.** The profitability inflection on Ascend is real — meaningful but expected. The structurally bigger development is Huawei's published roadmap committing to **indigenous HBM**. HBM was the clean choke point in the export-control regime: yield problems at SMIC could be ground out over years, but HBM required SK Hynix or Samsung. If Huawei's HBM design works at any usable spec, the parallel-stack thesis on `themes/china-tech.md` strengthens — China is no longer just catching up on the same stack, it's building a different stack along the bottleneck axis. Updates `themes/china-tech.md`, `themes/semiconductors.md`.

5. **Power constraint hardens: PJM auction shortfall + FERC colocation rules now propagating into 2026 PPA negotiations.** PJM's December 2025 capacity auction came in 6,623 MW under reliability target (data centers ~5,100 MW of the surge). PJM interconnection queues are >8 years. FERC's Dec-18-2025 order forced PJM to publish data-center colocation rules — three transmission service options + reformed behind-the-meter generation rules. Read alongside Talen–AWS (17yr/$18B/1.92 GW Susquehanna nuclear PPA) and TMI–MSFT: behind-the-meter and direct-power-plant deals are no longer one option among many — they're the *only* delivery path on AI buildout timelines. The orbital-compute LOI in (1) is the canary at the exotic end of the substitution curve. Updates `themes/energy-systems.md`.

## Mind-changers ⚠️

- **Anthropic–Colossus is the partial trigger for "compute supply dynamics broke from the linear hyperscaler model"** on `themes/ai-compute-capex.md`. Not a full mind-change — capex is still flowing through MSFT/GOOG/META/AMZN balance sheets — but the unit-of-account is now "GPU-megawatts under contract" across hyperscalers, specialty clouds (CoreWeave, Crusoe), rival labs' spare capacity (xAI), and orbital LOIs. The question shifts from "is hyperscaler capex sustainable" to "is the *meta-constraint* (HBM + power + grid) tight enough to keep compute scarcity priced into the curve regardless of who funds the capex."

The China-tech development in (4) connects: while the *macro* China story is balance-sheet-recession (monetary stimulus failing because the private sector is paying down debt), the *industrial-tech* China story is the parallel stack — state-directed capital flowing into SMIC/Huawei/HBM independence regardless of whether household demand recovers. **The two stories run on different clocks.** Demand-side macro is multi-decade workout; supply-side tech is national-security capex with a much shorter decision cycle.
