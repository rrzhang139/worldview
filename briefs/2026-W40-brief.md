# Weekly Brief — 2026-W40

*A quiet-on-structure week after W39's heavy one. Three developments update a theme question; the rest is carry-forward.*

## Top developments this week

1. **FERC rejects parts of PJM's data-center backstop auction and PJM suspends it within a day.** The auction (6.8 GW of new 2028/29 capacity, $555/MW-day weighted-average cap, offers due Sept 30–Oct 21) was sunk by disputes over cost allocation, transmission-owner exit rules, LSE collateral (NOVEC alone ~$2B) and co-op opt-outs; FERC Chair Swett called it a "deeply flawed, eleventh-hour procurement mechanism." No new date. **Updates** `themes/energy-systems.md`: the market-design route to marginal MW just failed on schedule, leaving DOE emergency orders (W39's 7th order) as the only mechanism actually moving. PJM still forecasts +30–34 GW of large load by the early 2030s.

2. **Beijing weighs letting ByteDance and Alibaba buy Nvidia's RTX Pro 5500 — and admits domestic chips have gaps.** Reportedly ~1M units for ByteDance, order deadline Sept 30, Nvidia shipping ~500K/quarter from late December; Huawei's Ascend 950PR at similar price judged insufficient. It's a workstation GPU, nothing is approved, and US licensing is unclear (chips weren't discussed at the summit). **Updates** `themes/china-tech.md`: first marginal softening of the self-sufficiency line since Nvidia's zero-China guide. Paired with Nvidia/AMD lobbying to keep China chip limits out of the defense bill (`themes/ai-policy-regulation.md`) — Congress is the only live venue for tightening.

3. **Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Sept 28): same $2/$10 price, ~30% fewer tokens per task, >30% faster.** Third straight frontier release competing on cost-per-task rather than headline price. **Updates** `themes/ai-models-economics.md`, favoring the efficiency-curve read over raw scaling.

## Mind-changers ⚠️

- **PJM backstop auction suspended (item 1)** — flagged in `themes/energy-systems.md`: reinforces, rather than reverses, the "regulatory improvisation beats RTO reform" read; the auction was the designed alternative.
